Why this decision

Slack and Microsoft Teams support the same enterprise collaboration capability for substantially the same user population. Teams has broader adoption, lower incremental cost and meets the required security controls.

Capability coverage: Teams covers messaging, channels, meetings and external collaboration.
Critical operations: No unique critical capability depends exclusively on Slack.
Dependency precondition: Migrate 14 workflow integrations and archive regulated channel history before contract exit.
Contract timing: Complete migration before the January 31 renewal date.
